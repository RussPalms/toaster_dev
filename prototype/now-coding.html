how it works

  
Now Coding listens to the audio and rebuilds it as live code, as if someone were writing the song in Strudel right now:

  
    
    
  • Beat: finds kick, snare and hi-hat hits, works out the tempo and where each bar starts, then learns which of the 16 steps in a bar each drum plays. Those become $: s("…") lines in Strudel's mini-notation.
    • 
    
  • Harmony: reads which of the 12 notes are loudest in each bar to name the chord, the bass note and the key.
    • 
    
  • Performance: a part that drops out gets commented out, and comes back when it returns. A big drop after a breakdown gets a blue screen.
    • 
  

  
It's a live reconstruction for show, not an exact transcription. It does best on music with a steady beat.
